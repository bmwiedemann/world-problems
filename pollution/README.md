## Problem

Some synthetic chemicals do not break down: PFAS, microplastics, some pesticides and flame retardants. They are now present in soil, rainwater, wildlife and human blood everywhere on earth.

Because they persist, the stock keeps growing even if emissions stop. The decision to release them is effectively permanent.

Harm is diffuse, delayed and hard to trace to one emitter, so no one is liable and nothing stops. The cost is paid by people who never chose it.

Several of these substances disrupt hormones and are suspected in falling sperm counts and fertility. See [health](../health/) and [demographics](../demographics/).

Air pollution from combustion already kills millions per year. See [energy](../energy/).
