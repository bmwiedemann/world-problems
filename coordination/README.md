## Problem

Most problems in this project share one shape. The cost of acting falls on whoever acts, the benefit is shared by everyone, and the damage of not acting is spread thin and arrives late.

So the rational move for each actor is to wait for the others. Emissions, fish stocks, aquifers, antibiotics and arms races all follow this pattern.

There is no authority above states. Treaties are agreements between parties who each keep the option to defect, and defection is usually cheap.

Decisions are made by people with four-year horizons on problems with hundred-year horizons. Costs now, benefits after the decider is gone. See [politics](../politics/).

Discounting the future is rational for an individual and ruinous for a species.

This is the bottleneck. Nearly every technical solution elsewhere in this project already exists on paper and is not deployed because of this problem.
