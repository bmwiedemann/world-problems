## Problem

Wild animal and insect populations are shrinking. The causes are habitat loss, land-use change, pesticides, invasive species and warming, acting together.

Ecosystems supply pollination, soil formation, pest control, water filtration and fisheries at no cost. There is no affordable technical substitute at that scale. See [food](../food/).

The ocean absorbs a large share of our CO2 and acidifies in the process. This hits shell-forming plankton and corals at the base of marine food webs, which billions of people depend on for protein. See [climate change](../climate-change/).

Ecosystems tend to hold and then flip rather than degrade smoothly, so warnings come late.

Extinction is irreversible on human timescales. This is the one problem here where mistakes cannot be corrected later.
