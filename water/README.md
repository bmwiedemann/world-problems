## Problem

Water is renewable but locally finite. In many regions withdrawal already exceeds recharge.

Large aquifers are mined, not used: the Ogallala, the North China Plain, north-west India. They took millennia to fill and are being emptied within a lifetime. Some coastal ones take in seawater once drawn down and never recover.

Glaciers and snowpack act as the reservoir for the rivers of Asia and South America. They are shrinking, so flow shifts from a steady melt to flood-and-drought. See [climate change](../climate-change/).

About 70% of withdrawals go to irrigation, so a water shortage arrives as a [food](../food/) shortage.

Rivers and aquifers cross borders and belong to nobody. Upstream dams and pumping are already a source of conflict. See [politics](../politics/) and [war](../war/).

Desalination and long-distance transport work, but both trade water for [energy](../energy/).
