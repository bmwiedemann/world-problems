## Problem

Antimicrobial resistance is rising while the antibiotic pipeline is nearly empty, because antibiotics are cheap and meant to be used rarely. Surgery, chemotherapy, intensive care and childbirth all assume working antibiotics.

Dense cities, industrial livestock and air travel make spillover of animal viruses more likely and much faster to spread. COVID-19 was mild compared to what is biologically possible.

Cheap gene synthesis and [AI](../ai/) design tools lower the skill needed to engineer a pathogen. Defence is slower than attack here.

Health systems must serve more old people with fewer workers. See [demographics](../demographics/).

Chronic disease from diet, inactivity and [pollution](../pollution/) already consumes most health spending.
