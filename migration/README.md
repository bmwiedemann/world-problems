## Problem

Heat, drought, sea-level rise and harvest failure will make parts of the tropics and subtropics much harder to inhabit. See [climate change](../climate-change/), [water](../water/) and [food](../food/).

Population growth is concentrated in Africa and South Asia, while ageing rich countries shrink. See [demographics](../demographics/).

The resulting gradient in wages and safety will move hundreds of millions of people. Not as one event, but as steady pressure over decades.

Rich countries need immigrants to sustain their workforce and pensions, and their voters increasingly reject them. Both things are true at once.

Receiving societies respond with backlash, which pulls [politics](../politics/) toward the short term exactly when long-term decisions are needed.
