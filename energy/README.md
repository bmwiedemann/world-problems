## Problem

Global energy consumption has seen an exponential growth.
If this were to continue, it would soon surpass the amount of available energy (in some decades?).

Fossil fuels are problematic, because the CO2 emissions cause [climate change](../climate-change/)

Waste heat sets a hard ceiling: any energy used on earth ends up as heat, independent of the source.

Building non-fossil energy needs large amounts of metal, and grid-scale storage more so. See [resources](../resources/).

Thermal plants need cooling water, hydro needs rivers, and both are affected by drought. See [water](../water/).
