## Problem

Most western countries have a fertility-rate below the replacement-rate of 2.1 so population would be shrinking if there were no immigration. Some developing countries are getting there as well.

This causes a shift in demographics where a growing percentage of the population is 50+ years old.
Older people go into retirement, leave the workforce and need support by the society.
Letting fewer and fewer younger people support a growing number of old people is not sustainable.

Immigration can fill the gap in numbers, but the sending regions are the ones under most climate and food pressure, and receiving voters resist. See [migration](../migration/).

More old people also means more care needed from fewer workers. See [health](../health/).

Falling fertility has causes beyond choice, including possible chemical ones. See [pollution](../pollution/).

## Solutions

* encourage people to have more kids
