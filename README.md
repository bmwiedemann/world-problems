## What is this about

The world is full of small problems, but behind them
are often a small number of big and hard problems.

This project is about exploring these problems and think about possible solutions.

## Structure

there are subdirectories with individual topics
that link to other topics where appropriate.

## Topics

* [ai](ai/) - machines becoming more capable than us
* [biodiversity](biodiversity/) - losing species and the services they provide
* [climate-change](climate-change/) - warming from our greenhouse gas emissions
* [coordination](coordination/) - why known solutions do not get done
* [demographics](demographics/) - fewer young people supporting more old ones
* [economics](economics/) - work, ownership and income when machines do the work
* [energy](energy/) - growing demand, and the cost of meeting it
* [food](food/) - feeding more people from strained land
* [health](health/) - resistant bacteria, new pathogens, ageing patients
* [incentives](incentives/) - rewards that produce bad decisions
* [information](information/) - not being able to tell true from fake
* [migration](migration/) - people moving away from places turning hostile
* [politics](politics/) - short terms, self-interest and long-term decisions
* [pollution](pollution/) - substances that never break down
* [resources](resources/) - materials running short or held by few
* [war](war/) - weapons that can end large parts of humanity
* [water](water/) - using fresh water faster than it returns
