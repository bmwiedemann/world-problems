## Problem

We mine the easy deposits first. Ore grades fall over time, so each ton of metal needs more rock moved, more [energy](../energy/) and more [water](../water/).

Electrifying everything needs copper, lithium, nickel and rare earths far faster than mines can open. From discovery to production takes 10 to 20 years, so a shortage cannot be answered quickly.

Mining and especially refining are concentrated in few countries, which makes supply a political lever rather than a market. See [politics](../politics/).

Some materials are dissipated rather than consumed and cannot be recovered: phosphorus spread on fields and washed into the sea, helium lost to space.

Recycling can supply a stable stock but not a growing one.

Cheap materials are an assumption in nearly every proposed solution elsewhere in this project. See [economics](../economics/).
