## Problem

Yield growth of the major cereals is slowing while demand keeps rising.

High yields rest on two mined or synthesised inputs. Nitrogen fertiliser is made from natural gas, so its price follows [energy](../energy/). Phosphate is mined from a few deposits and has no substitute at all. See [resources](../resources/).

Topsoil erodes faster than it forms. Salinisation and falling water tables take irrigated land out of production. See [water](../water/).

The world eats a handful of genetically narrow crops. Heat above flowering thresholds cuts yields sharply, and simultaneous harvest failure in several breadbaskets is plausible within this century. See [climate change](../climate-change/) and [biodiversity](../biodiversity/).

Most countries import calories from a small number of exporters. When harvests fail, exporters ban exports first, which turns a regional shortfall into a global one. See [politics](../politics/).

Stocks cover weeks, not years.
